???+ quote "Abstract" 
    The Kompetenzzentrum 3DK (3DK Competence Center) at the CeraTechCenter in Höhr-Grenzhausen, Germany, combines the expertise of three project partners, with the aim of adapting existing printing processes to ceramic materials and developing new printing technologies. In this context, among other things, the project is also investigating the adaptation of contour crafting for refractory castables.
