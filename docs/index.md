# Introduction 

<a title="Last Update"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/Last%20Update-2025--12--28-a2ad00></a> <a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dan-auer/3dcp.fyi/3dcp.bib" title="Download Dataset ↓" target="_blank"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/Download%20Dataset-3dcp.bib%20(9%20MB)-e37222></a>

Relax, this isn't another review paper on 3D Concrete Printing (3DCP). Instead, we've curated an exhaustive database that offers a detailed look at the State-of-the-Art in 3D-printing in the construction industry, mostly, but not exclusively, involving cementitious materials and extrusion-based manufacturing. To guarantee the dataset's quality and consistency, we've taken the extra step of manually curating each entry over the last six years.

You can explore the database by the <a href="https://www.3dcp.fyi" title="Entries in Database"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/4654-Articles-98c6ea style="vertical-align: middle;"></a> using the search, by the <a href="https://www.3dcp.fyi/keyword" title="Authors in Database"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/1029-Keywords-98c6ea style="vertical-align: middle;"></a>, or by the <a href="https://www.3dcp.fyi/author" title="Authors in Database"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/8525-Authors-98c6ea style="vertical-align: middle;"></a>. Additionally, you can navigate the collection based on specific months and years using the date index <a href="https://www.3dcp.fyi/date" title="Dates in Database"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/since-1997--02-98c6ea style="vertical-align: middle;"></a> .
Each entry is enriched with direct links to the original publications, DOIs, and a suite of academic search engines to facilitate further research. The project is open-source, licenced under <a href="http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/" title="Licence"><img src=https://img.shields.io/badge/Licence-CC%20BY--NC--SA--4.0-999999 style="vertical-align: middle;"></a> and we welcome contributions from the community to help keep the database current and comprehensive.
