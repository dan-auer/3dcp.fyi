???+ quote "Abstract" 
    This paper presents recent experimental test results on the bond behavior of 3D printed concrete specimens with embedded conventional steel rebars, focusing on different material behavior depending on the material layer orientation. The investigations include pull-out tests according to European standards (ÖNORM EN 10080:2005) as well as compressive strength tests according to ÖNORM EN 12390-1. Specimens with different material layer layouts were produced. A systematic determination of the shear parameters shows that the layer orientation of the printed concrete has a significant influence on the bond-slip behavior. In addition, preliminary tests were carried out, showing the effect on the bonding conditions depending on the duration of the print interruption during the insertion of the rebars.
