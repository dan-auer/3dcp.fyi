???+ quote "Abstract" 
    In recent years, the country has vigorously developed the construction field, and 3D printing of concrete-based buildings has great market potential. In this paper, an extruder structure of concrete 3D printing equipment is designed, which can ensure the high efficiency and stability of the printing equipment. The simulation analysis is carried out to verify its feasibility.
