???+ quote "Abstract" 
    A nozzle (print head) for a laboratory 3D printer is developed and tested. The device permits continuous printing of a three-layer wall (a sandwich structure) using two types of concrete mixtures: structural concrete for the central layer; and heat-insulating concrete for the thin outer layers.
