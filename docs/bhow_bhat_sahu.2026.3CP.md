???+ quote "Abstract" 
    3D concrete printing is an innovative construction method that uses computer-controlled machines to build 3D structures. It brings several advantages compared to traditional construction, like reducing waste, saving time and money, and allowing for intricate designs. This review explores the current status and advancements of 3D concrete printing in the market. It examines different printing methods, techniques used, industries adopting this technology, and its global reach. The analysis covers the driving factors behind its growth, hurdles faced, potential opportunities, and future trends. The review concludes by proposing recommendations for further research and development in this field, aiming to enhance the capabilities and applications of 3D concrete printing. Overall, it provides a comprehensive overview of this emerging technology's current state, shedding light on its promising prospects and areas for improvement.
