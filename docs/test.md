# Welcome 

<div class="grid" markdown>

Relax, this isn't another review paper on 3D Concrete Printing (3DCP). Instead, we've curated an exhaustive database that offers a detailed look at the State of the Art in 3D-printing in the construction industry, mostly, but not exclusively, involving cementitious materials and extrusion-based manufacturing. To guarantee the dataset's quality and consistency, we've taken the extra step of manually curating each entry over the last six years. The project is open-source, licenced under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and we welcome contributions from the community to help keep the database current and comprehensive.<br><br><br>[:ti-download: Download `*.bib` data (9MB), last updated on 2025-12-27](https://github.com/dan-auer/3dcp.fyi/releases/download/25-06-08/3dcpfyi.bib){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
