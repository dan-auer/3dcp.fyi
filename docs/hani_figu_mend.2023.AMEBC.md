???+ quote "Abstract" 
    Free forms in architecture by using robots and digital manufacturing processes give prospect promises that increase additive manufacturing (AM) interest. The fabrication with sustainable materials as earth-based composites started with new construction systems taking advantage of computation and digital fabrication. Companies such as “WASP” had efforts into developing technologies to build a more sustainable shelter, whereas earth has been widely used as a traditional material [4].
