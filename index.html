3dcp.fyi A Manually Curated, High-Quality Database on the State of the Art in 3D Concrete Printing

Maintained by the Chair of Concrete Structures & Professorship of Digital Structures with Mineral Materials of the Technical University of Munich · Latest publication
Relax, this is not another review paper. Just an exhaustive dataset on 3DCP covering articles by authors from to , indexed by keywords, main areas with categories and subcategories, and cross-references. Free, no account needed.
